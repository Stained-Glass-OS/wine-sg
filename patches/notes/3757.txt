CORE review: security (crypt32 messages sign and encrypt; the signing and encryption themselves are unchanged -- the same CSignedMsgData_Update / CryptEncrypt calls, now fed in pieces; the BER normalizer sits in front of the decoder for every message with an indefinite length). Docs: learn.microsoft.com CMSG_STREAM_INFO, CryptMsgOpenToEncode (streaming is not used with CMSG_HASHED; with streaming pszInnerContentObjID must be NULL or szOID_RSA_data; CMSG_BARE_CONTENT_FLAG: no outer ContentInfo), CryptMsgUpdate.
Encode, signed (CSignedEncodeMsg_StreamOut): first update sends 30 80 {ContentInfo: OID signedData, A0 80} 30 80 {version, digestAlgorithms} 30 80 {OID data, A0 80} then 24 80 (cbContent = CMSG_INDEFINITE_LENGTH) or 04 <cbContent> (known length, the update bytes follow raw); each update with data sends 04 <len> <bytes> in the indefinite case; the final update sends 00 00 (string) 00 00 ([0]) 00 00 (content info), the encoding's certificates / CRLs / signer infos (taken from the message encoded with no content, so they are the bytes the one-piece message has), and the closers. Detached: the inner content info is 30 0b <OID data> and no content is sent. The hash and signature are the unchanged ones. Known cbContent: more content than declared fails E_INVALIDARG, fewer at the final update fails CRYPT_E_STREAM_INSUFFICIENT_DATA (GUESS: the codes). The callback of a message is called as the application's own: a NULL one faults and the update fails with STATUS_ACCESS_VIOLATION (as the existing data message does).
Encode, enveloped (CEnvelopedEncodeMsg_StreamOut): 30 80 {OID envelopedData, A0 80} 30 80 {version, recipientInfos} 30 80 {OID, content encryption algorithm} A0 80, then the cipher text as 04 <len> chunks (block ciphers: only whole blocks until the final update, which adds the padding), then six (ten without the bare flag) 00 octets. GUESS: Windows' exact framing (definite lengths when cbContent is known, chunk sizes); the output is valid BER that this decoder, and any BER decoder, reads.
Decode: CDecodeMsg_Update turns BER into DER (sg_ber_normalize) before decoding a message that has indefinite lengths: constructed OCTET STRINGs become one string, and so does the constructed [0] of an encrypted content info (a SEQUENCE { OID, SEQUENCE, [0] }); a message that is not BER we understand is left as it was (same errors as before). Single-byte tags only; nesting 24.
Unverified / not done: the streamed HASHED message (the documentation says it is not streamed) still sends nothing; no definite-length framing for enveloped; the decode side's own streaming (callbacks while decoding) is patch 3758.
Gate fastb-msgstream: signed (one update, three, many odd sizes, empty, definite length, bare, detached, 65 KB, tampered output must not verify) and enveloped (RC2, 3DES, RC4, empty-ish, bare, 65 KB) round-trips through the non-streamed decoder (signature verifies, content equal, plain text absent), the streamed signer info equals the one-piece message's, cbContent errors, NULL callback, update after final, BER data message with two chunks, truncated BER fails; 21 mutants caught (CHUNK_HEADER_MISSING TRAILER_MISSING EOC_MISSING BARE_IGNORED DETACHED_CONTENT_SENT DEFINITE_LEN_WRONG OVER_NOT_CHECKED UNDER_NOT_CHECKED SIGNED_NO_HASH ENV_CIPHER_NOT_FINAL ENV_REMAINDER_LOST ENV_NO_CHUNK_HEADER ENV_NO_RECIPIENTS ENV_ECI_EMPTY_KEPT NO_FINAL_FLAG HEADER_EVERY_UPDATE NORMALIZE_OFF NORMALIZE_NO_STRINGS NORMALIZE_NO_IMPLICIT NORMALIZE_NO_EOC_CHECK; wrongly accepting: SIGNED_NO_HASH, the tampered-output check).
