7801 server, win32u: another process's child window keeps what it draws (DevToys 2 black client area)

David's dev box 2026-11-15 (gaps2's open row): DevToys 2 -- WPF with Mica hosting WebView2 -- showed a black
client area (with sg-deskcomp: the desktop through it). With 7785/7786 the app runs; repro in Xvfb (copied
prefix with SG's DXVK + WebView2 157) still black in about half the runs. Two causes:
1. The GPU process's DC for its own window ("Intermediate D3D Window", the DirectComposition target) under
   WPF's top-level kept WPF's old X window after WPF made a new one with alpha (Mica): dce.c updates a DC's
   visible region every time only for another process's window, not for our window in another process's
   top-level. BadPicture (RENDER error 143) for every frame, nothing drawn. Now updated each time.
2. WPF's drawing over its whole client (the WebView2 host is WS_EX_TRANSPARENT, so not clipped) was flushed
   from its window surface over the page. A child window of another process than the surface's (it draws
   itself straight into the X window) is now left out of the surface, frame and all, as a swap chain's area
   is (server clip_pixel_format_children; transparent children are passed over only when nothing of another
   process or with a pixel format is in them); the child's process tells the surface's process when it
   moves, appears or goes (WM_WINE_CLIENT_SURFACE with delta 0, sent after the window lock is released --
   sent under it, win32u's user_check_not_lock aborted msedgewebview2).
Plain GDI from another process into a child is thereby never painted over by the parent's process (Windows
would let the parent paint over it until the child repaints); composition/swap-chain content is what Windows
keeps above, and is what this is for.
Gate: test/xprocdc-gate.sh (make test-xprocdc): host top-level with a transparent child, a render process's
red child in it drawn once; red after the host's full fill, red drawn again after the host turns glass
(32-bit X window), red after another fill. Mutants SG_MUTANT_FOREIGN_CHILD_IN_SURFACE (server),
SG_MUTANT_FOREIGN_CHILD_NO_NOTIFY (win32u window.c), SG_MUTANT_XPROC_TOP_STALE_DC (win32u dce.c) caught.
DevToys 2 in Xvfb: 6/6 runs render (0/8 before in the last batches); under sg-deskcomp the page shows with
its Mica translucency.
