Cause: the server already treats HKCU\Software as shared (5949), but a 64-bit process's KEY_WOW64_32KEY opens and creates go through kernelbase's own walk (open_key -> open_wow6432node_parent -> open_subkey), which steps into any existing Wow6432Node subkey at every level -- at HKCU\Software too, once anything had made HKCU\Software\Wow6432Node.
Fix: open_subkey does not step into Wow6432Node below a user's HKCU\Software (\REGISTRY\USER\<one name>\Software; Software\Classes and HKLM keep their redirection). Mutant SG_MUTANT_HKCU_WOW32KEY.
