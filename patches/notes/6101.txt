6101 win32u (depth pass: with Slack over KeePassXC 2.8.0, KeePassXC's taskbar hover thumbnail showed Slack's page). The taskbar takes a thumbnail with PrintWindow(PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT) and falls back to a screen BitBlt when it fails. print_other_process_window (1660) opened the window's process with PROCESS_DUP_HANDLE to push a section into it; KeePassXC's process DACL (it keeps other processes out of its memory) refuses that, so PrintWindow returned FALSE for it every time (a PrintWindow probe from another process: ok=0, all black). Now, when the section cannot be given, WM_WINE_PRINTWINDOW carries the requester's own handle in wparam and its process id in lparam bits 32-63, and the window's thread opens the requester (PROCESS_DUP_HANDLE on an ordinary process) and duplicates the section from it. The requester waits in send_message_timeout, so its handle lives until then. Real app: PrintWindow of KeePassXC with Slack over it -> KeePassXC's own pixels (e3e3e3/eff0f1). Gate test-printguard (a host that sets a protected process DACL without PROCESS_DUP_HANDLE, a green window half covered by another; PrintWindow from another process: ok and green on both halves); mutant SG_MUTANT_PRINT_NO_PULL caught (2 FAIL).
