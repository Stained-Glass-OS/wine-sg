6187 dlls/windows.ui.xaml -- XAML program, Windows Terminal 1.25 (TermControl.xaml ForkedScrollbarTemplate, MIT source read for the template's part names and states).
buttons.c: RepeatButton (Delay/Interval DPs 250 ms, statics) clicks on press, a thread timer repeats while the pointer is over it, the release click is dropped; Thumb (IsDragging DP, DragStarted/Delta/Completed with DragStarted/Delta/CompletedEventArgs, CancelDrag). input.c: press begin/end (xaml_press_begin/end) separate from hover state, so a RepeatButton or Thumb keeps going off the element while captured; pointer coordinates from the messages (xaml_pointer_position). A button with a template no longer draws our face (button_render returns, as control_render does).
range.c: a templated ScrollBar on arrange: CommonStates Normal, NoIndicator/TouchIndicator/MouseIndicator from IndicatorMode, Expanded (mouse) or Collapsed; Horizontal/VerticalRoot and PanningRoot shown by Orientation; thumb length track*viewport/(range+viewport) (at least MinHeight), large decrease (track-thumb)*fraction; handlers on the parts' Click and DragDelta step Value; Value/Minimum/Maximum/ViewportSize/Orientation/IndicatorMode changes re-arrange it.
animation.c: KeySpline from four numbers separated by commas or spaces.
Gates: xamlrepeat (Delay/Interval defaults and markup, press/held/release clicks, drag events, templated face), xamlscrollparts (part sizes, steps, drag, states), xamlstates + spline check. Mutants: SG_MUTANT_REPEAT_BUTTON_ONCE, SG_MUTANT_REPEAT_RELEASE_CLICK, SG_MUTANT_THUMB_NO_DELTA, SG_MUTANT_TEMPLATED_BUTTON_FACE, SG_MUTANT_SCROLL_BAR_PARTS_STILL, SG_MUTANT_SCROLL_BAR_PARTS_INERT, SG_MUTANT_SCROLL_BAR_NOT_RELAID, SG_MUTANT_SCROLL_BAR_NO_STATES, SG_MUTANT_KEYSPLINE_SPACE_ONLY.
