6025 ntdll (coordinator row, Crashpad dump size): the TEB StackLimit of a thread stack. virtual_alloc_thread_stack committed the whole reserve and put the guard page and StackLimit at the bottom, so [StackLimit, StackBase) was the whole stack; Crashpad (Chromium, Electron, Edge, Dropbox, Spotify) reads that range from every suspended thread's TEB and captured ~7.9 MB of each 8 MB stack: Dropbox's dumps were 436 MB each and filled a 27 GB disk on qa4. Now, as on Windows: StackLimit = StackBase - max(commit size, 16 KB), a guard page under it, and the existing guard handler (grow_thread_stack) moves both down as the stack grows; check_write_access (read/pread/recvmsg/server replies that the kernel writes into user memory) grows the stack when it meets the thread's guard page, as Windows' fault handler does for kernel-mode writes; virtual_setup_exception grows page by page down to an exception frame that reaches past the guard page. Differences from Windows, on purpose: the pages under the guard page stay committed read-write (one region, as Windows' reserve is one region) because Wine's own code does not probe large frames (its __chkstk is a no-op), and a frame that steps over the guard page must not fault; the lowest page is mprotected no-access to stop a runaway stack; is_valid_frame accepts frames down to DeallocationStack. ntdll virtual test: 6 todo_wine checks of the stack layout now pass (committed 0x9000 after growth, the guard/commit regions) and lose their todo_wine.
Docs: learn.microsoft.com "Thread Stack Size" (reserve, commit, guard page), NT_TIB.
Gate stacklimit (test-stacklimit), 64 and 32: a new thread's StackLimit within 64 KB of its stack pointer and far from the whole reserve (default and 8 MB reserve), a 256 KB alloca's probes move it, recursion moves it, a guard page under it, ReadFile into the guard page succeeds and moves the limit, an endless recursion still ends in STATUS_STACK_OVERFLOW. Mutant caught: SG_MUTANT_STACK_LIMIT_BOTTOM (the old bottom limit).
Conformance vs before (64/32): ntdll virtual 0 = 0 (6 todo_wine removed), thread 0, exception 4 = 4 (64; a wow64 GetContextThread check flaked once), kernel32 thread/fiber/process 0, loader fewer failures, msvcrt misc, ucrtbase misc, dbghelp minidump 0. Other gates: jsstubs, vbsbuiltins, wsproto, olelink pass.
Left: pages under the guard page are not decommitted (Windows reserves them); stacks a program makes itself (RtlCreateUserStack, fibers) keep their own layout.
