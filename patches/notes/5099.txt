5099 netio.sys: WskSocket basic and stream sockets, WskListen (first part of gap-CORE item 1067; the datagram kind follows in 5100).
WSK_FLAG_BASIC_SOCKET gets the WSK_PROVIDER_BASIC_DISPATCH (control + close only); WSK_FLAG_STREAM_SOCKET gets WSK_PROVIDER_STREAM_DISPATCH: bind, accept, connect, listen, send, receive, disconnect, release, local/remote address, ConnectEx. WskListen is listen(SOMAXCONN) on a bound socket (STATUS_INVALID_DEVICE_STATE otherwise). A socket accepted on a stream socket is again a stream socket; one accepted on a listen socket stays a connection socket.
Docs: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/wsk/nc-wsk-pfn_wsk_socket (flag/dispatch table), nc-wsk-pfn_wsk_listen.
Unverified: the dispatch kind of a socket accepted on a stream socket (the pages do not say; we keep the stream table so the client's table layout matches); a basic socket's dispatch/context arguments are not checked to be NULL.
