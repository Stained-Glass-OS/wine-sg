RPCRT4_IsValidHttpPacket / RPCRT4_NextHttpHeaderField (items 584/585) had FIXME
"unimplemented type" for RTS commands 0x7..0xb and 0xe. Both now share one
size table taken from MS-RPCH 2.2.3.5 (open specification):
 Empty 7, NegativeANCE 9, ANCE 0xa: no data; Padding 8: ConformanceCount (<= 0xFFFF)
 then that many bytes; ClientAddress 0xb: address type (0 IPv4 / 1 IPv6) + 4 or 16
 byte address + 12 bytes padding; PingTrafficSentNotify 0xe: 4 bytes.
Behaviour change: an unknown command type (> 0xe) or a malformed Padding /
ClientAddress now makes the packet invalid (before, the validator guessed and
carried on, misparsing the rest). Known types keep their previous sizes.
Gate: test/rpcrt4-httprts-gate.sh is a host-compiled harness that cuts the two
functions out of the patched rpc_message.c (no RPC-over-HTTP proxy exists to
test end to end; no Wine test covers these paths). Three mutants fail it.
