5212 win32u: MNS_AUTODISMISS (gap-CORE 713).
Docs: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/winuser/ns-winuser-menuinfo ("MNS_AUTODISMISS: Menu automatically ends when mouse is outside the menu for approximately 10 seconds").
NtUserThunkedMenuInfo only said FIXME. track_menu now, when the top menu of the tracking has the style, remembers since when the cursor is outside every menu of the chain (menu_from_point on the cursor position, checked each time the loop goes idle) and ends the tracking (exit_menu, as a click outside would, TrackPopupMenu returns 0 with TPM_RETURNCMD) after 10000 ms; the idle wait is bounded by the time left. The cursor coming back over the menu restarts the time. The FIXME for MNS_AUTODISMISS is gone (MNS_DRAGDROP and MNS_MODELESS keep theirs).
Unverified: "approximately 10 seconds" is taken as exactly 10 s of the last continuous time outside; whether native restarts the time on any mouse movement or only on entering the menu; the style on a sub menu is ignored (only the top menu's counts); a menu bar (TrackMenu from the keyboard) is handled the same way.
Gate test/win32u-menu-autodismiss-gate.sh (64/32 bit, about 100 s each: plain menu stays, autodismiss ends at 10 s, the mouse inside restarts the time); mutants MENU_NODISMISS and MENU_NORESET shown failing it.
