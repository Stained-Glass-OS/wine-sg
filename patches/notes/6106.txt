Its frames draw their own title bar; the minimise/maximise/close buttons are a child window of class JBRCustomTitleBarControls (WS_CHILD|WS_VISIBLE, WS_EX_LAYERED|WS_EX_TRANSPARENT) filled by UpdateLayeredWindow (GDI+ lines). win32u gives a layered child a surface of its own; winex11's CreateWindowSurface -> set_window_visual -> create_whole_window made its X window at the root with the child's parent-relative rect: the buttons showed near the screen's top (xwininfo: 141x40+1111+0 at the desktop's level while the frame was at 10,20), stayed when the frame moved, and nothing showed in the title bar. Fix: create_whole_window and window_set_config put a child's X window in its top-level's whole window at the child's place there (mapped through NtUserMapWindowPoints to the top-level's client, plus its client offset in the visible rect); the X window gets an empty input shape (clicks reach the frame, which hit-tests the Win32 way: WS_EX_TRANSPARENT children are passed over); the top-level's surface GC is ClipByChildren once it holds such a child (it was IncludeInferiors and painted over it). PyCharm: buttons in place, minimise works by click.
