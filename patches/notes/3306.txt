3306 cabinet. XPRESS: [MS-XCA] plain LZ77. Checked against the specification's examples as remembered (alphabet 26 bytes, "abc" x 100 = ff ff ff 1f 61 62 63 17 00 0f ff 26 01) and by round trips. GUESSES: the framing of buffer mode for XPRESS (same 24-byte header as MSZIP, chunk stored when not smaller); raw mode = one stream over the whole buffer; the chunk size has no effect on the dictionary (each chunk is independent); Windows' own encoder produces different (equally valid) streams, so compressed sizes differ. NOT done: XPRESS_HUFF ([MS-XCA] LZ77+Huffman: its bit and byte interleaving could not be checked without reference streams) and LZMS: still ERROR_NOT_SUPPORTED. Interoperability with streams made by Windows is verified only by the specification examples above.
FDITruncateCabinet: uncompressed cabinets built by the test; files continued from/to another cabinet (iFolder 0xfffd..0xffff) are handled as the first/last folder; the data of the folders that stay is moved, not recompressed. GUESSES: a folder number past the last returns TRUE and changes nothing; a cabinet that continues in a next one loses that link (flags, names) because its last folder goes. Per-block reserve and checksums are untouched.
