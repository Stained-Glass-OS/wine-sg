6167 dlls/windows.ui.xaml -- XAML program Track B (ListView; listed as not done in 6164).
textinput.c: items_control keeps sel (a byte per item index), an anchor, SelectedItems (an xvector of ours whose added/removed callbacks select/deselect the item when the application changes it; rebuilt in item order after our own changes, guarded by syncing) and SelectionChanged handlers (ISelector slots 16/17). selection_set(index, ONLY/TOGGLE/RANGE/RANGE_ADD) snapshots the set, changes it and selection_commit sets SelectedIndex/SelectedItem to the first selected, refreshes SelectedItems and raises SelectionChanged (SelectionChangedEventArgs with AddedItems/RemovedItems vectors; also IRoutedEventArgs) when anything changed. Clicks: Single selects one; Multiple toggles; Extended: Shift range (Ctrl+Shift adds), Ctrl toggles, plain selects one. Keys (6164's handler): Single follows the focus; Extended follows unless Ctrl (focus alone), Shift ranges from the anchor; Multiple leaves it; Space: Multiple and Ctrl+Space in Extended toggle, Shift+Space ranges, else selects one. IListViewBase.SelectedItems (slot 6).
Horizontal panels: items_orientation makes the ItemsPanel template's content once (reset when ItemsPanel changes) and reads StackPanel.Orientation; horizontal items are measured with unbounded width and arranged left to right at the list's height; the list keys use Left/Right then (Up/Down unhandled). markup.c: ItemsStackPanel and VirtualizingStackPanel made as StackPanels (layout only; no virtualization).
