IDL:
- IHTMLControlRange (3050f29c-98b5-11cf-bb82-00aa00bdce0b) in mshtml.idl (so it is in the typelib).
- Dispids DISPID_IHTMLCONTROLRANGE_* in mshtmdid.h. Layout and dispids from the mingw-w64 headers.
New ctrlrange.c (HTMLControlRange):
- A DispatchEx with disp_tid IHTMLControlRange_tid and no prototype (named ControlRangeCollection, as TextRangeCollection is).
- It keeps its document and elements (node refs, with CC traverse/unlink).
- add() takes an IHTMLControlElement's element, once only; remove() and item() return E_INVALIDARG out of range; length.
- commonParentElement: the first element's parent, widened until it contains all.
- scrollIntoView: the first element's.
- select(): the document's nsISelection gets one range per element (SelectNode).
- query*/execCommand are the document's, after select(). Delete removes the elements themselves (removeNode): Gecko's delete command on a selected table only emptied it.
htmlelem.c: every HTMLElement answers IHTMLControlElement, forwarding to IHTMLElement2's members of the same names. IHTMLElement2::createControlRange creates a range (script reaches this one).
