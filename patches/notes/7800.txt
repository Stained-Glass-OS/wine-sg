7800 winex11/explorer: a framed Linux program's menus stay open (Steam's View/Friends/Games/Help)

David 2026-11-15: Steam's top menus showed "for only a single frame and then disappear". Steam's
menus are top-level X windows of their own that close when they lose the X focus. Three causes, all
reproduced with a stand-in (test/embedmenu-app.c) in Xvfb:
1. The taskbar framed the menu: the compositor lists a transient window once its main window, framed,
   is no longer an Xwayland top-level it shows; explorer framed it (reparented: the focus lost, closed).
   sg_embed.c is_popup(): never framed are windows typed _NET_WM_WINDOW_TYPE_MENU/DROPDOWN_MENU/
   POPUP_MENU/TOOLTIP/COMBO/DND/NOTIFICATION, and undecorated windows (_MOTIF_WM_HINTS) transient for
   another or skip-taskbar. SG_EMBED_QUERY answers 1<<47 for them; explorer gives them no button and
   remembers them while listed. Decorated dialogs are framed as before.
2. The click that opened the menu activated the frame afterwards (posted) and gave the focus back to
   the program's window: sg_embed_focus asks at the click's time (ICCCM: the server keeps a later focus,
   the menu's), retries with CurrentTime if the focus went elsewhere, and never takes it from the
   program's own menu (same _NET_WM_PID / WM_TRANSIENT_FOR / WM_CLIENT_LEADER, menu-like or OR).
3. Framing a window that went meanwhile ended explorer, the shell: X11DRV_check_error saw no async
   error (BadWindow on X_ChangeSaveSet came later, to Xlib's fatal handler). Every check in sg_embed.c
   syncs first (sync_check_error).
Gate: test/embedmenu-gate.sh (make test-embedmenu): transient, POPUP_MENU and override-redirect menus
stay open, top-level, shown, focused, no frame/button, no XDESKTOP; an unmarked window is still framed.
Mutants: SG_MUTANT_EMBED_FRAMES_MENUS, SG_MUTANT_EMBED_FOCUS_OVER_MENU, SG_MUTANT_EMBED_ERROR_UNSYNCED.
Compositor side (main): program_surface() lists and holds (2.5 s hidden) such windows too --
