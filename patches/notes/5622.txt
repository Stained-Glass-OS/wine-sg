Applies after 5619. crypt32 msg.c: the decoder keeps the parsed RecipientInfos (ktri, kari [1], kekri [2]) and the unprotected attributes of an EnvelopedData. CMSG_CMS_RECIPIENT_COUNT_PARAM counts every recipient of any choice; CMSG_RECIPIENT_COUNT_PARAM keeps counting key transport only. CMSG_CMS_RECIPIENT_INFO_PARAM (index) returns a CMSG_CMS_RECIPIENT_INFO of the right choice. CMSG_UNPROTECTED_ATTR_PARAM returns the unprotected attributes (CRYPT_ATTRIBUTES; CRYPT_E_ATTRIBUTES_MISSING when absent). After CMSG_CTRL_DECRYPT the recipient / encrypted-key / CMS recipient index parameters report the recipient used; before it, CRYPT_E_INVALID_MSG_TYPE-style failure as for other unset parameters. CMSG_ENCRYPT_PARAM returns whether the content is encrypted. Docs: CryptMsgGetParam, CMSG_CMS_RECIPIENT_INFO, RFC 5652 section 6.
