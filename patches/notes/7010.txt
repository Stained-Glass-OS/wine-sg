7010 msspellcheckingfacility (gaps4; HexChat 2.16.2: enchant's win8 provider found no CLSID_SpellCheckerFactory {7ab36653-...}, "init_enchant_provider returned invalid provider", no spell check). A new dll and include/spellcheck.idl, written from the public documentation; the vtable order was checked against mingw-w64's public-domain header.
- Backend: a unix lib that dlopens libhunspell-1.7.so.0, with a mutex per handle and iconv for non-UTF-8 dictionaries. Dictionaries are in /usr/share/hunspell (also /usr/local/share/hunspell and myspell). Without the lib or a dictionary there are simply no languages.
- debian/control Recommends libhunspell-1.7-0 and hunspell-en-us; main adds both to the image.
- Parameters use UINT64 pointers, so the wow64 table is the 64-bit one.
- Languages: "en_US" -> "en-US". A bare "en" resolves to en-US (another language to its own region, else its first variant); HexChat asks for "en".
- Check: word runs with inner apostrophes (' and U+2019); runs with digits are skipped, and so are chunks containing @ \ / _ or ://. Results: misspelled -> GET_SUGGESTIONS; autocorrect pair -> REPLACE; repeated word -> DELETE.
- Suggest: hunspell's suggestions; S_FALSE plus the word itself when the word is right.
- Ignore: session only. Add/Remove: persisted in %APPDATA%\Microsoft\Spelling\<tag>\default.dic (UTF-16 with BOM). Also .exc (excluded) and .acl ("from|to"), plus IUserDictionariesRegistrar files.
- The change event fires on Ignore, Add, Remove and AutoCorrect. There are no options.
- Old prefixes register the class on their next wineboot update.
- Gate spellcheck (64- and 32-bit probe, 22 checks; SKIPs without hunspell-en-us/libhunspell). Mutant SG_MUTANT_SPELL_ALWAYS_RIGHT (unixlib.c) caught (8 FAIL).
- HexChat: the input box underlines "helo wrld" in red.
