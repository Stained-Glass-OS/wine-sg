focus.c: an unhandled arrow KeyDown on the focused element (not a text box) looks for the scope: the nearest ancestor with the mode Enabled (Disabled first: none), or for Left/Right a CommandBar (xaml_is_command_bar). Candidates: the visible focus targets in the scope with their absolute bounds (summed offsets, RenderSize); those beyond the focused one in the direction, scored by the gap along it, a large penalty when they do not overlap on the cross axis, plus twice the centre offset across; the lowest wins, as Keyboard focus. Simplified from the documented XY navigation (no NavigationStrategy, no XYFocusUp/Down/Left/Right overrides, no auto-scrolling, no leaving the scope); ListView items do not take arrow keys yet.
