David 2026-11-15: "Scrolling in Firefox doesn't work."
sg_embed.c grabbed AnyButton on the frame (GrabModeSync) for the click that
activates it, replayed to the program. The wheel's buttons 4-7 went through it
too: each notch posted WM_X11DRV_SG_EMBED_ACTIVATE (the frame activated by a
wheel, which Windows never does), and while the frozen grab waited for the shell
to replay it, the wheel's XI2 smooth-scroll events (Firefox scrolls by XI2) were
the grab's. Now only buttons 1, 2, 3, 8, 9 are grabbed.
Gate: test/embedwheel-gate.sh (test-embedwheel): an XI2-scrolling stand-in,
framed, sees every notch; the wheel does not activate its frame; a click does.
Mutant SG_MUTANT_EMBED_GRAB_WHEEL fails it. Xvfb's XTEST wheel reached the
program even with the old grab; the activation is what it proves on Xvfb.
