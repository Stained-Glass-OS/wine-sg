Gate: test/crypt32aux-gate.sh (64+32). Mutants: SG_MUTANT_ASYNC_INTRES, _NOFREE, _REPLACE, _HANDLE, _GET; the forwards were mutated by hand in the spec (each retargeted to the ANSI/other function) and the gate failed each time.
Unverified against Windows: error codes of the async calls (ERROR_INVALID_HANDLE, ERROR_NOT_FOUND for an unknown name, ERROR_INVALID_PARAMETER for NULL), that a replaced parameter's free function is called at once.
Left stubbed: RegCreateHKCUKeyExU/RegOpenHKCUKeyExU (signature not certain), CryptEnumOIDFunction (enumeration semantics unverified), I_Crypt*Lru*, CryptExport/ImportPKCS8, CryptLoadSip, message-level calls (next batch).
