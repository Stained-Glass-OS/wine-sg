5201 gdi32 uniscribe: ScriptApplyLogicalWidth (gap-CORE 972) and ScriptJustify (973).
Docs: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/usp10/nf-usp10-scriptapplylogicalwidth and .../nf-usp10-scriptjustify.
ScriptApplyLogicalWidth: the logical widths of the characters of a cluster add up to that cluster's advance; a character that became several glyphs shares its width equally between the glyphs that are not zero width (the remainder goes to the last); the ABC black width is rebuilt as the new total minus the bearings; bad arguments (NULL arrays, a cluster index beyond the glyphs) give E_INVALIDARG (the docs do not say; Windows may fault).
ScriptJustify: documented priority kashida, then blanks (SCRIPT_JUSTIFY_BLANK and ARABIC_BLANK), then characters; the change is shared equally between the points of the chosen class, the remainder on the last, never below zero width; min_kashida limits how many kashida points are used; S_FALSE with the advances copied when nothing can be justified, as documented.
Unverified (no Windows output at hand): the exact distribution Windows uses (which glyph of a class gets the remainder, how the ARABIC_* sub-classes rank against each other - all ranked equal here), and the ABC rule. Results are only as exact as the docs' description.
