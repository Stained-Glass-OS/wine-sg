6522 wmvcore (coordinator's row (b): WM reader timing). Investigation: the 40 ms frames (GStreamer's default when the ASF has no extended stream properties' AverageTimePerFrame) and the late start (asfdemux seeks by the simple index, whose entries count presentation time from the preroll) were both artefacts of our writer before 6518, which added the Extended Stream Properties and fixed the index. With them, WMCreateSyncReader and WMCreateReader (OnSample, user clock) give 10 and 30 fps WMVs (video only, and with a WMA stream) their own frame count, times (+-1 ms) and durations. The readers themselves need no change for files that carry these objects (all WM9+ writers'); an ASF without them still gets GStreamer's 40 ms durations (not changed: Windows writers always write them).
Code: SG_MUTANT_WMVW_NO_FRAME_TIME hook in asfmux.c (writes 0 as the average time per frame).
