3304 dwrite justified alignment (CORE review). Layout-level justification: the room of a soft-wrapped line is shared equally by its blanks (U+0020, U+00A0, U+3000), else by the edges after kana/CJK ideographs. Everything about how Windows shares the room is a GUESS (equal shares; blanks win over ideographs; the opportunity tables of the text analyzer are not consulted; no kashida; tabs are not opportunities). Not done: GetClusterMetrics keep natural widths (Windows may report stretched ones); GetMetrics().width stays the natural widest line (Windows may report the layout width); DWRITE_TEXT_ALIGNMENT_JUSTIFIED with inline objects: objects keep their width, blanks around them stretch; vertical text.
