3299 mf/winegstreamer video capture (M-CAPTURE).
Tested only through the WINE_SG_FAKE_CAMERAS hook (videotestsrc, raw and JPEG-only fake cameras): enumeration, attributes, category filter,
type list/default, Start/Stop/Shutdown events, request-driven samples, YUY2/NV12 frames, restart with a new type, timestamps, idle CPU, no thread leak, 64+32.
NOT tested against real hardware (the host's /dev/video* are never opened): the /dev scan with VIDIOC_QUERYCAP, v4l2src device caps probing (stepwise
size/framerate ranges -> standard sizes and rates is a guess), v4l2src open/negotiation/streaming, camera unplug (error -> MEError), uvc metadata nodes skipped by device_caps.
Guesses: Pause returns MF_E_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION (no CAN_PAUSE); the YUY2/NV12-only type list (no MJPG/RGB offered); the sample timestamps start at
pipeline running time; samples are dropped without an outstanding request; MFCreateDeviceSource without a symbolic link is E_INVALIDARG; unknown link is ERROR_FILE_NOT_FOUND.
Not done: audio capture sources (create_object stays E_NOTIMPL), IAMVideoProcAmp/camera controls, IMFMediaSourceEx::SetD3DManager, FrameStep/GetVideoSwapchainHandle/H.264 profile/mmdevapi stream_init of the same group.
