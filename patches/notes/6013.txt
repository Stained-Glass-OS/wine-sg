6013 kernel32/kernelbase K-MOUNT: volumes mounted on folders. SetVolumeMountPointW (and A): the folder must exist and be empty (ERROR_DIR_NOT_EMPTY), the mount point needs its trailing backslash (ERROR_INVALID_NAME), the volume a \\?\Volume{...}\ name of a mounted drive (ERROR_INVALID_PARAMETER); the folder becomes a mount-point reparse point (junction) to the drive's root, recorded under HKLM\Software\Wine\MountedFolders. GetVolumeNameForVolumeMountPoint of such a folder gives the drive's volume; FindFirst/Next/CloseVolumeMountPoint(A/W) enumerate a volume's mount points (relative paths with the trailing backslash, ERROR_NO_MORE_FILES); DeleteVolumeMountPointW/A removes the reparse point and leaves the empty folder. The folder is opened without its trailing backslash (that would follow the junction).
Docs: learn.microsoft.com SetVolumeMountPoint, DeleteVolumeMountPoint, FindFirstVolumeMountPoint, GetVolumeNameForVolumeMountPoint.
Gate kmount (test-kmount), 64 and 32: errors, mount, attributes, the volume's files through it, volume name, enumeration, delete, folder stays, volume untouched. Mutants caught: SG_MUTANT_MOUNT_DELETE_KEEPS, SG_MUTANT_MOUNT_NO_ENUM.
Left: volumes without a drive letter (Wine's mount manager gives every volume a letter), mount points across prefixes.
