Fix to 3741 (a new patch, not an edit): IsDestinationReachable treats 127/8 and ::1 as reachable with no route (QOC LAN flag, speed 0; GUESS: Windows reports the loopback adapter speed). Gate fastb-sens now runs the probe three times: on the host, and in two user+network namespaces made with unshare -rn (no route; a dummy interface with a default route), each with its own wineserver, so the outcome no longer depends on the machine's routing table. Mutants CON_ALWAYS (IsInternetConnected always S_OK), CON_NEVER, SENS_LOOP_ROUTE (loopback needs a route) all caught; the 3741 mutants SENS_ALWAYS/NOARGS/NOQOC unchanged. Without usable user namespaces the gate runs the host check only and says so.
