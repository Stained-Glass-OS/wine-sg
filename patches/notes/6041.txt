6041 riched20 (RE-TOM "Left": TOM2). Ranges and the selection answered only ITextRange/ITextSelection; TOM2 callers asking for ITextRange2/ITextSelection2 (RichEdit 7+ users) got E_NOINTERFACE.
Approach: one vtable per object kind. trvt2 (ITextRange2Vtbl) holds the range's ITextRange methods, the ITextSelection methods of a range (range2_*), then the TOM2 methods (tom2_*); tsvt2 (ITextSelection2Vtbl) holds the selection's methods, then the same TOM2 methods. The old trvt/tsvt are replaced and the vtable identity checks (tom_range_editor) follow.
The tom2_* methods work through ITextRange calls on the object, so they serve both kinds:
- GetCch, GetCount (1), GetSubrange(0), GetDuplicate2, Get/SetFormattedText2;
- GetGravity (tomGravityUI), SetGravity (0 ok, 1-4 E_NOTIMPL, others E_INVALIDARG);
- GetStartPara, Find (the other range's text via FindText), GetChar2 (offset from the start, S_FALSE outside the story), GetRect (GetPoint of start and end), GetText2/SetText2 (flags ignored).
- Tables, math, inline objects, drop caps, URL, more subranges and ITextFont2/ITextPara2 are E_NOTIMPL.
The ITextSelection methods of a range act on the range:
- GetType: IP or Normal; GetFlags 0.
- Move{Left,Right,Up,Down}: a non-empty range collapses to that side first, counting one; extend moves that end.
- HomeKey/EndKey: StartOf/EndOf. TypeText: SetText, then collapse to the end.
Gate tom2range (test-tom2range): the QI of a range and the selection, GetCch/GetCount/GetSubrange/GetText2/GetDuplicate2/GetChar2/GetGravity/SetGravity/GetStartPara/SetText2/Find, and a range's GetType/MoveRight (move and extend)/TypeText/HomeKey. Wine's tom.h values for gravity and text flags are not in the tree; literals are used with comments.
Mutants caught: SG_MUTANT_TOM2_NO_RANGE2 (1), SG_MUTANT_TOM2_CCH (4).
