6501 win32u, winex11 (field report: blank taskbar hover thumbnails for Dropbox, Opera). PrintWindow shows a window's swap chains.
The taskbar (explorer systray.c sg_print_capture), Task View/Alt+Tab (vdesktop.c capture_thumb) and dwmapi's DwmRegisterThumbnail all use PrintWindow(PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT) (cross-process since 0076), which copied only the window surface: a GPU-presented window was white (Chromium/Electron), Office's Vulkan panes white. No sg-shell change needed.
Fix: print_window_to_section (the window's own process) computes the parts its surface leaves to swap chains (surface rect minus clip region, inside its shape) and calls winex11's new X11DRV_SG_PRINT_FRAMES escape: those parts come from XCompositeNameWindowPixmap of the window when a compositor redirects it (sg-deskcomp: covered windows too) or from the X window where Wine's system region says it is visible; then the last frames of the process's own child swap chains (1523's shown list) from the redirected offscreen windows that keep them.
Gate: xproc-child (same as 6500): print of the child's frame, of the top-level's frame (its GDI child over it), and composited while covered by another window.
Mutant caught: SG_MUTANT_PRINT_NO_FRAMES (3 FAIL lines). (A no-pixmap mutant was not caught: in the shell desktop under sg-deskcomp the visible-region path reads the redirected window's pixmap too; kept for other compositors.)
Conformance (user32 win, msg, winstation; 64/32): see PROGRESS.md / commit.
Not covered: OpenGL child windows of the process's own are holes (pixel format) and taken from X as shown, not from their offscreen drawables.
