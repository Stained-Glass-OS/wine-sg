5222 gdi32: EMF playback of the escape and UFI records (gap-CORE 40, PARTIAL).
Docs: [MS-EMF] (learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/windows_protocols/ms-emf) RecordType enumeration, EMR_EXTESCAPE / EMR_DRAWESCAPE (Type, Size, iEscape, cjIn, Data: "passes arbitrary information to a printer driver"), EMR_NAMEDESCAPE (iEscape, cjDriver, cjIn, DriverName, Data), EMR_FORCEUFIMAPPING, EMR_SETLINKEDUFIS, EMR_GLSRECORD, EMR_COLORCORRECTPALETTE, EMR_COLORMATCHTOTARGETW.
PlayEnhMetaFileRecord logged "type %d is unimplemented" for these. Now EMR_EXTESCAPE calls ExtEscape(iEscape, cjIn bytes), EMR_DRAWESCAPE calls DrawEscape, EMR_NAMEDESCAPE calls NamedEscape with the driver name (a record whose sizes do not fit in its Size, or an odd driver-name size, is dropped quietly). EMR_FORCEUFIMAPPING and EMR_SETLINKEDUFIS (font mapping by UniversalFontId, a checksum of the font file) are accepted and ignored: Wine's fonts have no UFI, the font mapper keeps using the LogFont, which is what the records fall back to when no installed font has that id. EMR_STARTDOC (0x6B) is not a record of [MS-EMF] and is ignored.
STILL NOT DONE (the FIXME stays): EMR_GLSRECORD / EMR_GLSBOUNDEDRECORD (the OpenGL data's format is not specified), EMR_COLORCORRECTPALETTE and EMR_COLORMATCHTOTARGETW (need a WCS colour transform, which Wine does not have; ColorCorrectPalette is itself a stub). So item 40 is PARTIAL and escalated.
Unverified: the end-to-end effect of the escapes (DrawEscape and NamedEscape are themselves stubs in gdi32; ExtEscape of a display DC reaches the display driver, which handles few escapes). The gate looks at the playback's trace lines.
