9950 explorer systray.c (cal1, 2026-11-26; David: "when you click the time it should bring up a nice calendar just like in Windows"):
sg_toggle_calendar(bar) -- SgCalendarToggle's lparam (or sg-calendar64.exe --show --bar N when no copy runs) names the bar whose
clock was clicked; sg-shell's sg-calendar (0.1.0-313) opens against that bar, on its display and edge. The other displays' bars
(7751, sg_second_proc WM_LBUTTONUP) now open it from their clocks too -- before, their clocks did nothing (found in a two-head VM).
Gates: clockcal (the main bar names Shell_TrayWnd; mutant SG_MUTANT_CALENDAR_NO_BAR), mmtaskbar (displays 2 and 3's clocks name their
Shell_SecondaryTrayWnd, the main one Shell_TrayWnd; mutant SG_MUTANT_SECOND_CLOCK_DEAD). mmtaskbar runs a compositor: QA VM only.
