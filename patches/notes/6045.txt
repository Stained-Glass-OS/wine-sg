6045 windowscodecs (G-WIC "Left": CreateImageEncoder). IWICImagingFactory2::CreateImageEncoder was a FIXME returning E_NOTIMPL. Programs that save Direct2D drawing through WIC failed: screenshot and drawing tools, and the SaveToImageFile pattern of the Direct2D samples.
New imageencoder.c (IWICImageEncoder):
- render_image makes a device context on the encoder's ID2D1Device. The size and format come from WICImageParameters (PixelFormat, Dpi, Left/Top as the drawing offset, PixelWidth/Height); without parameters, a bitmap's own pixel size, format and dpi; otherwise the image's local bounds at 96 dpi.
- It draws the image into a TARGET|CANNOT_DRAW bitmap, copies that into a CPU_READ bitmap, maps it and wraps the pixels in a WIC bitmap (BitmapImpl_Create, resolution set).
- Formats: B8G8R8A8 (ignore/premultiplied/straight -> 32bppBGR/PBGRA/BGRA) and R8G8B8A8. Others are WINCODEC_ERR_UNSUPPORTEDPIXELFORMAT.
- WriteFrame: SetSize, SetResolution, then WriteSource (the frame converts to its own format). WriteFrameThumbnail: frame SetThumbnail. WriteThumbnail: encoder SetThumbnail.
- No device, or a NULL image or frame: E_INVALIDARG.
The d2d IIDs used are local copies, not GUIDs taken from the uuid library.
Gate wicimageenc (test-wicimageenc; Xvfb, D3D11 hardware or WARP, D2D1CreateDevice). A 32x16 bitmap of three colour areas is written as a PNG and decoded again. Checks: size and three pixels; WriteThumbnail on PNG returns the encoder's WINCODEC_ERR_UNSUPPORTEDOPERATION; parameters for the 16x8 area at (16,8) give a 16x8 green PNG; CreateImageEncoder without a device and WriteFrame of nothing are E_INVALIDARG.
