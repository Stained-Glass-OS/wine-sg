6500 win32u (field report: 0586's child-window case; Chromium/Electron/CEF windows go white after a repaint). Another process's swap chain keeps its window's area.
Reproduced on the host (Xvfb, lavapipe): a render process presents red with Vulkan into a host's child window (0586 offscreen + copy) and into its top-level (6046 in place); the host's RedrawWindow (erase and all) wiped both white (screen pixel FF0000 -> FFFFFF). 6046's in-place window is painted over too: x11drv's window surface GC includes inferiors.
Cause: the browser's window surface covers the swap chain's area and is flushed over it; only the presenting process knows the frame (1523's reshow runs in the flushing process, for its own frames).
Fix: as for a window of our own with a pixel format, the window's area is left out of its surface. win32u_vkCreateWin32SurfaceKHR on another process's window sends (notify) WM_WINE_CLIENT_SURFACE (pid, +1; -1 at vkDestroySurfaceKHR) to the window's thread; the server's UIPI filter lets it through to a window of more integrity (Electron's sandboxed GPU process is low integrity: without it, ERROR_ACCESS_DENIED and nothing changed for real Electron); WND keeps per-process counts (4 slots; processes that ended are dropped when a message comes); while any is >0, set_window_pos sends SET_WINPOS_PIXEL_FORMAT, so the server's surface region (clip_pixel_format_children / get_surface_region) leaves the client area out; the window's own GDI children still draw over it. A DC of such a window (no pixel format of its own) still paints the surface (dce.c), which leaves the area out -- GDI under a swap chain stays unseen, as with Windows' compositor.
Gate: xproc-child (test-xproc-child; Xvfb + lavapipe, pixel checks; covered/composited part with sg-deskcomp). DXVK's own frames do not reach a virtual X server's screen here (DXVK on lavapipe or the host's NVIDIA: black), so the probe renders with Vulkan directly, which takes the same winex11 path.
Mutants caught: SG_MUTANT_FOREIGN_SURFACE_PAINTED (child repaint wiped, top-level repaint wiped, print white: 6 FAIL lines), SG_MUTANT_UIPI_CLIENT_SURFACE (the probe's renderer runs at low integrity: 6 FAIL lines).
Real app: Electron 30 (page red, window background blue) in the shell desktop under sg-deskcomp, DXVK: PrintWindow(PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT) of its window from another process gives the page (ff0000); before, white (ffffff).
Regression: xproc-still, xproc-vk, vkchild, glasspixel, printwindow, dwmthumb, fastb-dwmthumb, glthumb, glthumbgray, thumbbar pass; glassvk fails the same as before (desktop backdrop expectation, noted in 6046). user32 win/msg/winstation conformance: see 6501.
Not covered: without a desktop compositor (SG_DESKCOMP=0), a swap chain's area uncovered by another window shows what covered it until the next present (before: the browser's white); with sg-deskcomp (the default) windows keep their pixmaps. OpenGL into another process's window (wined3d's GL path) does not post the message (wglSetPixelFormat on another process's window fails in win32u).
