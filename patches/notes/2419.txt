2419 crypt32: CryptMsgCountersignEncoded, CryptMsgCountersign, CryptMsgVerifyCountersignatureEncoded(+Ex) (were FIXME stubs returning FALSE), CryptEnumOIDFunction (stub), CMSG_CTRL_ADD_SIGNER_UNAUTH_ATTR for decoded signed messages.
Signatures are the SDK's. Countersignature form: a detached signed message over the signature value; signer info is what CMSG_ENCODED_SIGNER gives (no authenticated attributes unless the countersigner asks for some: then contentType + messageDigest are added and the digest is the hash of the signature value). Only one countersigner per call (E_INVALIDARG otherwise). CryptMsgCountersign works on decoded messages only (the added attribute does not change the message's own encoding). Verification supports CAPI keys (RSA); the CNG-only (ECC) signature path is not covered. CMSG_VERIFY_SIGNER_NULL = digest check only (guess of the documented meaning).
Unverified against Windows: whether Windows adds authenticated attributes by default to countersignatures, CryptEnumOIDFunction's flag/argument errors (E_INVALIDARG) and whether DEFAULT entries are included (they are here), CRYPT_E_AUTH_ATTR_MISSING when the digest attribute is absent.
Not done: CryptExportPKCS8/CryptImportPKCS8 (Wine lacks the CRYPT_PRIVATE_KEY_INFO / PKCS8 import-params types and the PKCS_PRIVATE_KEY_INFO codec: a batch of its own), CryptLoadSip, I_Crypt* internals, Reg{Open,Create}HKCUKeyExU (signatures uncertain).
Gate: test/crypt32cs-gate.sh (64+32). Mutants caught: SG_MUTANT_CS_{NODIGEST,NOSIG,NOATTRHASH,REPLACE} (msg.c), SG_MUTANT_ENUM_ONEONLY (oid.c). Conformance: crypt32 message/msg/oid 0 failures.
