6023 d3d9 G-SHARED (ledger i0000, the D3D9Ex part): D3D9Ex shared handles. A render target (CreateRenderTarget/Ex), an offscreen plain surface or a texture (its first level) in D3DPOOL_DEFAULT made with a NULL *pSharedHandle gets a legacy shared handle: the D3DKMT resource Direct3D 11 makes for a shared texture (system memory in win32u; private runtime data in d3d11's layout: WINE_D3D_SHARED_MAGIC, TEXTURE2D, the adapter LUID, pitch, content size, a D3D11_TEXTURE2D_DESC with the DXGI format), so Direct3D 11 (OpenSharedResource) and DXGI (GetSharedResourceAdapterLuid) open it. One made with a handle opens that resource (its size and format must match: D3DERR_INVALIDCALL) as a reader. The creator hands its pixels over (GetRenderTargetData, or a lock of a plain surface) at EndScene, Present/PresentEx and Issue(END) of an event query, when somebody has the resource open; a reader takes a newer version (UpdateSurface) at BeginScene and when it opens. Formats: A8R8G8B8, X8R8G8B8, A8B8G8R8, A2B10G10R10, A16B16G16R16F, A32B32G32R32F, R5G6B5, A1R5G5B5, R32F, G16R16F, R16F, A8, L8 (others: D3DERR_INVALIDCALL with a FIXME). New dlls/d3d9/shared.c; d3d9 imports gdi32 (D3DKMT).
This is the wined3d path: on machines where the d3d payload's DXVK provides d3d9/d3d11 the sharing is DXVK's.
Docs: learn.microsoft.com "Feature Summary (Direct3D 9 for Windows Vista)" (shared resources), IDirect3DDevice9::CreateRenderTarget/CreateTexture pSharedHandle, ID3D11Device::OpenSharedResource.
