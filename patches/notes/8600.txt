8600 dlls/winepulse.drv/pulse.c pulse_stream_connect + pulse_write -- bugs1, bug 1 (Media Player audio hiccups, David's NVIDIA desktop, 1080p H.264/AAC).
Measured with a private PipeWire null sink (WirePlumber, device monitors off) and a recording of its monitor: every glitch was a winepulse underflow. Pulse held tlength = 3 periods (30 ms); ~27 ms more waited locally (pa_held_bytes) for the audio_client_timer thread. Instrumented timer: the waits are pa_stream_update_timing_info's round trip (pipewire-pulse main thread, 10-40 ms) and NtSetEvent through the wineserver (up to 67 ms), plus sleep overshoot. RT priority for the audio threads (sudo chrt, diagnostic) helped (140->31 glitch blocks under load) but cannot cover the server waits.
Fix: render tlength = min(client buffer, 100 ms); EARLY_REQUESTS instead of ADJUST_LATENCY (pipewire-pulse: with ADJUST it set latency (tlength-2*minreq)/2 = 30 ms quantum; without either, 60 ms; with EARLY, minreq = 10 ms). The post-underrun silence prebuffer is capped at 3 periods (it was bounded by tlength). A/V: position is winepulse's own period clock; the data at the server minus the padding stays constant once started, so the lag does not grow (pace gate run). Volume is applied at write time, so a volume change now takes up to the buffer (dsound: 80 ms) to be heard.
David's render, headless weston on the host NVIDIA GPU, 40 s: underflows 19/5/13 before, 1/1 after.
Gate pulsefeed (test-pulsefeed): private PipeWire, a DirectSound tone, the Wine process SIGSTOPped 30 ms every 400 ms for 8 s: 0 underflows and no gap in the recording; before 20-21 underflows. Mutant SG_MUTANT_PULSE_SHORT_TLENGTH (20 underflows, 97 gap blocks at 40 ms).
