8800 dlls/appxdeploymentclient/deploy.c remove_package_work, package.c RemovePackageWithOptionsAsync -- bugs2, store walk 2026-11-11 (an MSIX uninstall left Packages\<family>\LocalCache; Windows removes it).
Cause: remove_record deleted the package's files, aliases, shortcuts, classes and record, never the user's app data (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<family>, made by windows.storage.applicationdata 3050/3065/7717) nor the settings containers (HKCU\Software\Wine\ApplicationData\<family>). RemovePackageWithOptionsAsync ignored its options ("nothing to preserve").
Fix: remove_package_work reads the family first (not for frameworks or resource packages); after a successful removal, unless RemovalOptions_PreserveApplicationData, and only if no record of the family is left in the caller's view (own hive, then the machine's: another version staged or registered, or the machine's copy of a user's own record), it deletes that folder (delete_tree, long paths) and the settings key. Updates call remove_record directly and keep the data. Family names with path characters or "."/".." are refused.
Not done: a machine package removed by SYSTEM for all users leaves each user's data (Windows deletes it per user); drop_user_record could do it at the user's next start.
Gate pkgdata (test-pkgdata): Preserve keeps data+settings; an update v1->v2 keeps them; removing v2 takes Packages\<family> (LocalState, LocalCache, TempState) and the settings, leaves another family's folder; a failed removal takes nothing. Mutant SG_MUTANT_AX_REMOVE_KEEPS_DATA fails the two removal checks.
