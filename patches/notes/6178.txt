dxgi factory.c: the factory answers IDXGIFactoryMedia. CreateSwapChainForCompositionSurfaceHandle: E_INVALIDARG without a handle; otherwise CreateSwapChainForComposition (composition.c: Direct3D 11, GDI read-back) and the swap chain keeps a duplicate of the handle in a process list (newest first; removed when the swap chain goes). CreateDecodeSwapChainForCompositionSurfaceHandle stays E_NOTIMPL. New private IWineDXGISurfaceHandles (winedxgi.idl, uuid b5d3ad91-81e3-409d-84da-01807c20ad13) on the factory: swapchain_from_handle finds the swap chain for that handle or another handle to the same section (NtCompareObjects; ntdll imported), with a reference taken only while it has others. The handle's section is not written to; cross-process consumers are not served (Windows Terminal's out-of-process content mode is not on by default).
windows.ui.xaml panel.c: SwapChainPanels are listed; SetSwapChain takes the swap chain's IWineDXGICompositionSwapChain (others: FIXME, not shown); SetSwapChainHandle looks the handle up through CreateDXGIFactory1 of an already loaded dxgi.dll (xaml does not import dxgi), again at each paint until found. render.c: after each island paint, xaml_swap_panels_present tells each panel's swap chain the island and the panel's offset (set_target, which draws the last frame at once); a collapsed or removed panel's swap chain is told no window. Presents in between draw straight into the island (composition.c). Elements over a SwapChainPanel are drawn under its frame.
