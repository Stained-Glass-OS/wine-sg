6099 winex11 (depth pass on wined3d/llvmpipe after 6097: Slack 4.52.182's sign-in window was all white with GPU compositing; fine with --disable-gpu; Discord and Obsidian fine). Chromium's GPU process makes a D3D11 swap chain on the browser's top-level (CreateSwapChainForHwnd on Chrome_WidgetWin_1, another process's window); wined3d sets an OpenGL pixel format on it from the GPU process. Three faults, found with a two-process probe (a host window, a renderer presenting red three times then still):
1. create_gl_drawable chose offscreen rendering (DC_GL_CHILD_WIN) when needs_client_window_clipping said so -- the window has children (Chrome Legacy Window) and something covered part of it (KeePassXC over Slack's window when the drawable was made). For another process's window this process has no x11drv_win_data: detach_client_window did nothing, so the redirected offscreen window stayed mapped inside the browser's X window over the client area, and present_gl_drawable copied to the top-level at (0,0) without the client offset. Now gl_needs_offscreen: another process's top-level (same DPI) gets the client window in place (create_client_window's foreign path), as vulkan.c's foreign_toplevel does since 0586.
2. The browser's window surface was flushed over the frames (its white background): win32u_set_window_pixel_format fails for another process's window, so its surface region kept the client area. Now the drawable sends 6500's WM_WINE_CLIENT_SURFACE (pid, +1; -1 when the drawable is freed) to the window's thread, as win32u's Vulkan surfaces do.
3. With the client area left out, a window with no frame and no painting child (a bare popup) had an EMPTY surface clip region; window_surface_set_clip hands that to x11drv_surface_set_clip as rects with count 0, which meant "no clipping": the whole surface was put over the frames. An empty region (rects given, count 0) now clips everything (XSetClipRectangles with no rectangles); NULL still clears the clip. This also applies to Vulkan's 6500 case with only WS_EX_TRANSPARENT children.
