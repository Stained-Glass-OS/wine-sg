Field report 2026-10-17: audio drifting behind the picture in a long film, a seek re-aligning it. quartz's DirectSound renderer runs on the system clock and took every late sample as continuous, so a device slightly slower than the clock let the lag grow. Past 80 ms late it leaves out that much of the sample (less 20 ms). Not gated: Wine's quartz tests are not built in the release tree and the drift needs a slow device; mutant define SG_MUTANT_AUDIO_DRIFTS kept for a later gate.
