Not a recent patch: an interaction of 3714 (thumbnail thread idle: 50 ms rounds while pictures change, 1 s rounds after ten rounds with nothing to do) and 5500's gate, which changes the source after a quiet spell and reads the destination 600 ms later. The probe prints COLORREFs: the failing "pixel 0000ff" is RED, the old picture -- the change had not been picked up yet (expected failure rate ~40%; measured 3/8 on the dev tree, also failing without 6119).
Fix: idle rounds 200 ms (busy >= 40 rounds, i.e. thumbnails repainting over each other for 2 s, keeps 1 s). fastb-dwmthumb: 3 visible idle thumbnails for 6 s: 30 ms of CPU, 0 destination repaints (< 5% limit).

The destination is invalidated for a hidden one too. Not deterministically testable; no mutant for that half.
