5098 netio.sys: WskSocketConnect (gap-CORE item 1176) and WskGetNameInfo (item 1178).
WskSocketConnect: a worker creates the socket for the local address' family, binds it, connects it and completes the IRP with the new WSK_SOCKET (a connection socket, bound) as Information; Flags must be 0 and both addresses of one family (STATUS_INVALID_PARAMETER); a failed bind/connect closes the socket and maps the Winsock error (e.g. STATUS_CONNECTION_REFUSED).
WskGetNameInfo: GetNameInfoW on the address (copied; length <= SOCKADDR_STORAGE) in a worker; NodeName/ServiceName get the NUL-terminated name and Length; STATUS_BUFFER_TOO_SMALL if the caller's UNICODE_STRING (MaximumLength) is too small; STATUS_INVALID_PARAMETER for no names, a thread without a process, or a too long address; unresolvable host WSAHOST_NOT_FOUND/WSANO_DATA gives STATUS_NO_MATCH (the docs' code).
Docs: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/wsk/nc-wsk-pfn_wsk_socket_connect and nc-wsk-pfn_wsk_get_name_info.
Unverified/not done: OwningProcess/OwningThread/SecurityDescriptor are not used (the docs tie them to the caller's security context for sharing/resolution; the NULL-process "minimal privileges" account and the impersonation-token STATUS_INVALID_PARAMETER rule are security-model items, not implemented); STATUS_BUFFER_TOO_SMALL for a short buffer is our choice (the docs name no code); a datagram WskSocketConnect socket gets the connection dispatch table.
