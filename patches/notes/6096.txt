6096 winex11 (depth pass: Obsidian 1.14.5 on wined3d's OpenGL path -- machines without Vulkan, main 2026-10-24 -- its start window stayed black until the mouse moved over it). Reproduced with a probe: a D3D11 swap chain (wined3d, OpenGL) on a child window presents one red frame while its top-level is hidden, then the top-level is shown: black (shown first, then presented: red). A child window's GL drawable is offscreen (DC_GL_CHILD_WIN, XComposite-redirected; DC_GL_PIXMAP_WIN without XComposite) and present_gl_drawable copies it into the top-level's X window; into an unmapped window the copy is lost and nothing copies it again. Fix: create_gl_drawable marks the window with the property __wine_sg_gl_offscreen; present_gl_drawable records that a frame was copied (gl->presented); X11DRV_Expose (last event of a series, not the root window) walks the exposed window's descendants (any process: the property and the walk are server-side) and posts WM_X11DRV_SG_GL_RESHOW to the visible ones marked; the owning thread (X11DRV_WindowMessage -> sg_gl_reshow) copies the kept frame again with a cache DC. A window whose own class brush erases it over the frame still loses it (as before; Chromium's swap chain windows do not erase). Obsidian itself is NOT fixed by this: on wined3d its GPU process fails three times (Skia "Couldn't create surface", GrBackendFormat invalid on ANGLE/wined3d) and Chromium falls back to software compositing, whose frames the GPU process draws with GDI into the browser's window from another process; the browser's window surface flush then covers them (the window shows Obsidian's background colour). Logged as d0013. Gate test-glhiddenframe (Xvfb, wined3d forced with d3d11,dxgi=b; control + hidden + corners); mutant SG_MUTANT_GL_NO_RESHOW caught (2 FAIL). Regression: glthumb, glthumbgray, xproc-child, printwindow, vkchild, glasspixel pass.
