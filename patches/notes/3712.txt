3712 dwmapi: DwmRegisterThumbnail/UpdateThumbnailProperties/UnregisterThumbnail + new DwmQueryThumbnailSourceSize (registry per process, validation, in-process painting thread), DwmAttachMilContent/DwmDetachMilContent/DwmGetGraphicsStreamClient/DwmGetGraphicsStreamTransformHint (DWM_E_COMPOSITIONDISABLED, documented for Windows 7+), DwmSetPresentParameters (E_NOTIMPL, documented from Windows 8.1; the same page says "always S_OK" in its returns section, the description is the newer text).
Our series' dwmapi behaviour (0170 DwmFlush, glass/blur/cloak/colorization attributes, iconic bitmaps, timing) is untouched. Gates rerun against this tree and passing: vsync, dwmattrs, cloak, darkmode, glass, rounded, thumbbar.
NOT a compositor: there is no desktop compositor to draw a thumbnail over a destination (sg-deskcomp composites the windows, not these), so the process paints into the destination's client area ~20 times a second, detects when the destination repaints over it, and waits for the destination to redraw under a translucent picture before blending again. Effects: the thumbnail is only as good as PrintWindow(PW_RENDERFULLCONTENT) of the source (the surface the source last drew), it is clipped to the destination's client area, child windows of the destination can overdraw it, and a thumbnail of a minimized or hidden source keeps the last picture (nothing is repainted). Up to 16 visible thumbnails per process are painted.
GUESSES (undocumented): default destination rectangle when DWM_TNP_RECTDESTINATION is unset (the source's size at the client origin); E_ACCESSDENIED for a destination of another process; E_INVALIDARG for unknown property flag bits and for DwmQueryThumbnailSourceSize when the source window is gone; the rcSource rectangle is in the source's picture pixels (window or client area per fSourceClientAreaOnly); handle values.
