8701 dlls/shell32/shlexec.c sg_execute_via_broker -- apps10 (LibreCAD's NSIS installer, requireAdministrator, double-clicked on qa4's desktop: consent after exactly 180 s, 3/3 times).
Cause: the ShellExecuteEx thread (Explorer's desktop thread) waited WaitForMultipleObjects(ready, rundll32, 180000) without taking messages. rundll32 shell32,SgElevate started at once but sat in its start until that wait timed out (sg-elevate spawned 180 s later; winedbg started meanwhile blocked too): a new process's start sends to the desktop window.
Fix: sg_wait_serving_sent(): MsgWaitForMultipleObjectsEx(QS_SENDMESSAGE) + PeekMessage(PM_NOREMOVE|PM_QS_SENDMESSAGE) until a handle is signalled or 180 s pass. Only sent messages are delivered; posted and input messages stay queued (no re-entrancy into the desktop's input). Broker handshake, timeout and consent unchanged. qa4 with the patched shell32: consent after 5 s.
