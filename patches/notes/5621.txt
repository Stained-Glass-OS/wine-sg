Applies after 5619 and 5620 (uses the PKCS_PRIVATE_KEY_INFO / PKCS_ENCRYPTED_PRIVATE_KEY_INFO / PKCS_RSA_PRIVATE_KEY encoders and decoders of crypt32). ncrypt: new pkcs8.c. NCryptExportKey(NCRYPT_PKCS8_PRIVATE_KEY_BLOB, params): no parameters give the PrivateKeyInfo (RSA, NULL parameters, PKCS #1 RSAPrivateKey inside); NCRYPTBUFFER_PKCS_SECRET (a NUL terminated Unicode string) gives an EncryptedPrivateKeyInfo with PBES2: PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 10000 iterations, 16 byte random salt, AES-256-CBC (or AES-128/192 chosen by NCRYPTBUFFER_PKCS_ALG_OID; PBES2's own OID means the default) with a random iv. Other parameter types give NTE_BAD_FLAGS, NCRYPTBUFFER_PKCS_ALG_PARAM and unknown cipher OIDs NTE_NOT_SUPPORTED. NCryptImportKey reads both forms (the encrypted one with PBKDF2 using any of HMAC-SHA1/256/384/512 and AES-128/192/256-CBC) and goes on as an RSA full private blob. Docs: NCryptExportKey, NCryptBuffer, RFC 5208, RFC 8018.
