3714 dwmapi: cost of the thumbnail thread (coordinator review of 3712).
(1) Repaint rate: 50 ms rounds while a picture or property changes, 1 s rounds after ten rounds with nothing to do (also when thumbnails keep changing for 2 s, e.g. overlapping ones painting over each other), no wake-ups at all while no thumbnail is visible (event wait). Measured with 3 visible, non-overlapping thumbnails and an idle source: 190 ms of process CPU in 60 s (0.3%), 30 ms in 6 s; no WM_PAINT sent to the destination while idle. Source changes show up within about a second when idle.
(2) The thread ends when the last thumbnail is unregistered (the unregister wakes it) and when a visible thumbnail's destination window is destroyed (the thumbnail is dropped, so DwmUpdateThumbnailProperties on it is E_INVALIDARG). A destination destroyed while all its thumbnails are hidden is only noticed at the next call.
(3) A destination that paints itself (the gate's does, with WM_PAINT and an erase to white) is not repainted while nothing changes (0 repaints in 6 s and 60 s), and a thumbnail is painted again only when the destination has drawn over it or the picture changed, so there is no steady flicker. Overlapping thumbnails in one destination fight each other (each repaints over the other); they are slowed to one round a second after 2 s rather than prevented. NOT tested: sg-deskcomp (0744/0745) composites windows on the host's X server, which these gates do not run; the thumbnail is drawn into the client area like any GDI drawing, so the compositor sees it as window content.
Gate fastb-dwmthumb gained: one thread for 3 thumbnails, CPU below 5% over 6 s idle, no destination repaints while idle, thread gone after the last unregister and after DestroyWindow of the destination; mutants THUMB_SPIN (6040 ms of CPU) and THUMB_NOEXIT caught; the 3712 mutants are unchanged (rerun: see below).
