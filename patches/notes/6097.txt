Skia builds a format's sample counts from glGetInternalformativ(GL_NUM_SAMPLE_COUNTS) and starts the list with 1 only when that count is non-zero: with none, RGBA8 is not renderable even single-sampled and defaultBackendFormat is invalid. ANGLE builds the counts only for formats CheckFormatSupport calls MULTISAMPLE_RENDERTARGET; wined3d's d3d11 gave that flag only when 4 AND 8 samples worked (the feature level 11 hardware requirement), and llvmpipe does 4 (CheckMultisampleQualityLevels: 1 level at 4, none at 2/8) -> no format had it. Now the MULTISAMPLE_RENDERTARGET/LOAD (and RESOLVE for non-integer formats) flags are given when 2, 4 or 8 samples work; the counts themselves stay CheckMultisampleQualityLevels'.
