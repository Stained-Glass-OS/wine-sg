5086 ntoskrnl, winedevice: gap-CORE items 1086 (IoRegisterShutdownNotification), 1087 (IoUnregisterShutdownNotification); IoRegisterLastChanceShutdownNotification ("@ stub") implemented with them.
Docs: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/wdm/nf-wdm-ioregistershutdownnotification (one IRP_MJ_SHUTDOWN per registered device at system shutdown), -ioregisterlastchanceshutdownnotification (after the file systems are flushed), -iounregistershutdownnotification.
Not as Windows: delivery rides on the service shutdown control services.exe sends at wineboot --shutdown (programs/winedevice/device.c forwards SERVICE_CONTROL_SHUTDOWN to the new ntoskrnl export wine_ntoskrnl_shutdown_driver), one driver at a time; no file systems are flushed in between, so "last chance" only means after the other devices of the same driver; the registration is not removed after the IRP is sent; the IRP is sent to the registered device itself, synchronously.
