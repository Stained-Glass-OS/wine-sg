Field report 2026-10-17 #12 (focus / keyboard "not mapped"). Found with the Alarms & Clock gate's lost Alt+F4: Wine's fg and X focus were right, the keys reached the window, but F4 came as WM_KEYDOWN and Alt's menu opened. Two causes, both with slow low-level keyboard hooks (explorer's Win keys + sg-dictate's, chained cross-process): (1) win32u call_hook sent to the next hook's thread with SMTO_ABORTIFHUNG only, so the waiting hook thread took the next MSG_HOOK_LL inside the wait: hook calls nested, completed LIFO, keys delivered last-first (same for WH_MOUSE_LL). Now SMTO_BLOCK. (2) server queue_keyboard_message judged SYSKEY from desktop_shm->keystate, updated only when a key is queued after the hooks; now desktop->input_mods tracks L/R Alt and Ctrl at input time (reset from set_key_state async resyncs; initialised with the desktop). Gate althook (test-althook): a 40 ms LL hook chaining to explorer's, 'qwerty' + Alt+F4 three times; mutants SG_MUTANT_LL_CHAIN_NESTS (2/3 fail) and SG_MUTANT_MODS_AFTER_HOOK (fails; Alt+F4 lands on the desktop) caught. shellkeys gate passes; sg-shell clock gate passes without its title-bar workaround.
