3725 uxtheme: lifetime of buffered animations (from RECONCILE-CLOUD bug 1; idea of the IsWindow check from stubs-7 patch 5507).
Bug in 3713: animation_timer kept `any = TRUE` for an animation whose window was destroyed, so the thread timer ran every 15 ms for ever (probe: 200 WM_TIMER in 3 s) and the animation was never freed. Fix: the timer frees animations with !IsWindow(hwnd) (kills their timer) and kills itself when none is left; BufferedPaintUnInit frees the calling thread's animations (the handle then gives E_INVALIDARG; the BufferedPaintUnInit behaviour is a GUESS, documentation only says it closes the thread's buffered painting).
Gate fastb-uxanim gained: timer messages while the window lives (>=3 in 200 ms), after DestroyWindow at most 2 in 1.5 s and process CPU below 150 ms; after BufferedPaintUnInit at most 1 tick in 0.5 s and End on the handle is E_INVALIDARG. Mutants UX_DEADWND (101 ticks) and UX_UNINIT (34 ticks) caught.
Conformance uxtheme 64+32: 0 failures (22241 tests), same as stock.
