6009 t2embed G-EOT (ledger i0099): Embedded OpenType (EOT version 0x00020001, the W3C submission's format): TTEmbedFont (the DC's font, GetFontData), TTEmbedFontFromFileA (a font file, a collection's face), TTGetEmbeddedFontInfo, TTLoadEmbeddedFont (AddFontMemResourceEx; a new family name rewrites the name table, ids 1/4/6/16), TTDeleteEmbeddedFont (RemoveFontMemResourceEx), TTEnableEmbeddingForFacename (recorded per face name under HKLM\Software\Microsoft\Shared Tools\t2embed; not consulted yet). Embedding levels from OS/2 fsType (restricted: E_FONTNOTEMBEDDABLE, EMBED_NOEMBEDDING), the more restrictive of the font's and the caller's for loading; TTEMBED_XORENCRYPTDATA (key 0x50), TTEMBED_SUBSET reported as TTLOAD_FONT_SUBSETTED. Errors and flags in t2embapi.h.
Docs: learn.microsoft.com t2embed functions; www.w3.org/Submission/EOT (format).
Gate eot (test-eot): embed the DC's font (structure size, magic 0x504c, font data at the end), XOR, restricted font refused, from file, info, load renamed and select it by the new name, delete, refuse a bad magic. Mutants caught: SG_MUTANT_EOT_IGNORE_PERMISSIONS, SG_MUTANT_EOT_NO_XOR, SG_MUTANT_EOT_NO_INSTALL.
Left: MTX compression (refused on load: E_ERRORDECOMPRESSINGFONTDATA), real subsetting (the whole font is embedded; TTEmbedFont's glyph list ignored).
