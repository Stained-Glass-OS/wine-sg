6015 win32u (for 6009): a font added with AddFontResource(Ex) or AddFontMemResourceEx is found by fonts created before it: realized fonts are cached by their LOGFONT, and one realized for a face name that was no font yet (a substitute) kept being selected after the font with that name came. Adding a font now forgets the cached realized fonts (unused ones are freed, ones in use stay with their DCs and are freed when released). Windows maps a LOGFONT again on selection.
