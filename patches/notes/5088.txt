5088 ntoskrnl: gap-CORE item 1088 (IoReportResourceForDetection).
Docs: learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows-hardware/drivers/ddi/wdm/nf-wdm-ioreportresourcefordetection (STATUS_SUCCESS / STATUS_CONFLICTING_ADDRESSES with ConflictDetected / STATUS_UNSUCCESSFUL for an invalid list; a DeviceList wins over a DriverList; a list with a Count of zero gives the claim back).
Not as Windows: only claims made through this routine conflict with each other (Wine has no PnP resource arbitration and no hardware, so a resource "needed for a PnP-enumerable device" never conflicts); only the first full resource descriptor of a list is read; the claim is kept in memory, not in the registry's ResourceMap; IoReportResourceUsage is still a stub. The descriptors are parsed with the real 4-byte packed 20-byte layout (the CM_PARTIAL_RESOURCE_DESCRIPTOR of Wine's ddk/wdm.h is not packed and is therefore the wrong size; it is not changed here).
